# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how **SummerlinCodes** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you interact with the Bot on Discord. This policy applies to all users of the Bot across all Discord servers and direct messages.

By interacting with the Bot, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, cease using the Bot immediately.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Discord Account Data
When you use the Bot, we may collect:
- **Discord User ID** — your unique, non-username Discord identifier
- **Username and discriminator** — displayed name at time of interaction
- **Server (Guild) ID** — the Discord server where the interaction occurred
- **Channel ID** — the channel where the command was issued
- **Message content** — the text you send to the Bot, including commands and prompts
- **Attachments** — files or images you upload in Bot interactions

### 2.2 Interaction Logs
We log:
- Timestamps of interactions
- Command names and parameters
- Response status (success, error, partial)
- Diagnostic information (operating system, Discord client version) for troubleshooting

### 2.3 Data NOT Collected
We do **not** collect:
- Your password or authentication credentials
- Private messages from other users (only messages directed at the Bot)
- Financial or payment information
- Location data
- Browsing history outside of Discord

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data for the following purposes:

1. **Bot Operation** — to process your commands and generate responses
2. **Diagnostics** — to troubleshoot errors and improve Bot reliability
3. **Analytics** — to understand usage patterns (command frequency, popular features)
4. **Moderation** — to enforce acceptable use and prevent abuse
5. **Legal Compliance** — to respond to legal requests or protect rights

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

- Interaction logs are stored securely and retained for **90 days** unless extended for debugging or legal reasons
- User IDs and message content may be retained longer if required for dispute resolution or legal compliance
- Data is stored on servers with access controls, encryption at rest, and encryption in transit (TLS)

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell your data. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

- **Discord API** — necessary for Bot operation (Discord's own Privacy Policy applies)
- **Legal requirements** — in response to subpoenas, court orders, or applicable law
- **Protection of rights** — to prevent fraud, abuse, or harm to users or the platform
- **Service providers** — hosting and infrastructure providers bound by confidentiality agreements

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of data we hold about you
- **Deletion** — request deletion of your interaction data
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate data
- **Opt-out** — withdraw consent by removing the Bot from your server or blocking it in DMs
- **Complaint** — file a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at individuals under 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover such data, we will delete it promptly.

## 8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted with a new effective date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
